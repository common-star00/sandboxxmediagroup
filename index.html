About Us

A creative hub for bold ideas.

SandboxX Media Group is a Canadian creative company built to support businesses, artists, entrepreneurs, and community projects with professional media services from concept to completion.

From video and sound editing to branding, design, photography, marketing, and campaign support, we create work that feels polished, purposeful, and unforgettable.
