About SandboxX

Where creativity plays with purpose.

SandboxX Media Group is a Canadian creative media company built to help brands, artists, businesses, and communities look professional, sound unforgettable, and move with purpose.

Our Mission

Our mission is to bring bold ideas to life through powerful media, storytelling, design, sound, video, photography, marketing, and creative production. We create polished content that helps people stand out in a digital world.

The Founder

SandboxX was founded by Alex Simpson, a creative business owner with a background in private investigation, close protection, media production, video editing, sound editing, and community-focused entrepreneurship.

His style blends discipline, strategy, humour, anime-inspired imagination, street intelligence, loyalty, and cinematic ambition — creating a company that feels both professional and unforgettable.

Skull & Scorpion

Skull & Scorpion is a SandboxX affiliate focused on bold photography, cinematic visuals, brand storytelling, portraits, events, and creative media with edge.

The style is gritty, sharp, dark, confident, and memorable — built for people and brands that do not want to look ordinary.
